---
title: Standards
description: Compact expert briefs that steer a review or a build.
---

A standard is one markdown file about one specialty: accessibility,
internationalization, security, the workflow, logic, intent, principles. It reads
like the core of what
a senior specialist knows, broad rather than deep, written to steer an agent
rather than to enumerate checks. Standards hold knowledge, not procedure.
Steps belong to the skill that equips them.

They live in `standards/` at the repo root. Dev builds by the principles
and the workflow; brawl equips the rest as lenses. Two shared rules,
`finding.md` and `severity.md`, shape and score every finding. Each
standard has Core, Sources, Checks.
